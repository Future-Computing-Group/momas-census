How to code a paper

Code from the title and abstract only, for the setting the paper's method or analysis assumes, not settings it mentions in passing (an example task, a motivation sentence).

Focus
Agent: the contribution improves or analyses the performance, decisions or internals of one agent or one team, treating the other parties as its environment (wins more, negotiates better, best-responds). Interaction: the contribution concerns the joint outcome or the rules between the parties (efficiency, welfare, fairness across parties, equilibrium properties, collusion, incentive compatibility, mechanism, market, protocol or institution design, governance). A learning-agents paper whose analysis is the outcome they reach together is interaction. One developer training one model is agent. No decision-maker only if the paper has no learner, model or analyst at all.
Owners
The setting the method is meant for, even if the experiments simulate it. Several if the agents are built to face decision-makers that other parties own or that act for themselves (other organizations' or users' agents, humans, bidders, applicants, voters, firms, self-interested agents whose incentives a designer sets, an attacker). Otherwise one if one party trains, runs or deploys every decision-maker (a single model, a team built by one developer, self-play producing one policy, equilibrium computation as analysis). Otherwise not fixed.
